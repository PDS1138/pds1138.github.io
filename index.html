About

I have worked on both sides of the avionics integration boundary: developing embedded flight-control and test systems at equipment-provider level and integrating avionics and flight-control systems at aircraft/system-integrator level.

My work spans system requirements, functional definition, interfaces, embedded hardware/software, BIT and testability, onboard maintenance, verification, integrated laboratory testing, and aircraft integration. Current hands-on engineering extends into embedded C, STM32-based controls, sensors, electronics, electromechanical design, and mechanical prototyping.
