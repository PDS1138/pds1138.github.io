Problem / Product Goal
Automate a physical cooking process

Develop a self-contained controller that senses plant temperature and automatically positions a physical mechanism rather than depending on repeated manual adjustment.

System Architecture
Sensor → control law → motor drive

Functional architecture partitions temperature sensing, controller logic, motor-position feedback, PWM motor drive, mechanical actuation, and operator interaction.

Detailed Engineering
STM32 + embedded C + PI control

Designed electronics and PI control laws for a brushed DC motor using PWM, feedback loops on motor position and plant temperature, plus custom mechanical drive components.

Integration / Evidence
Prototype, instrumentation, measured response

Development progressed through bench electronics, control-law tuning, mechanical integration, oscilloscope/test instrumentation, and integrated operation with measured temperature response.

Product Goal
Court-side swingweight measurement

Targeted a compact standalone product around a $100 retail concept, optimized for portability, simple operation, and measurement of swingweight only.

Measurement Architecture
Gravity-restored pendulum + gyro

Determines oscillation period from rate-gyro zero crossings, then computes swingweight using known fixture mass, pivot geometry, and racket parameters.

Embedded Implementation
High-rate IMU + local UI

Firmware performs stillness qualification, gyro-bias handling, high-rate sampling, zero-crossing detection, period calculation, calibration storage, and local display/user input.

Engineering Focus
Accuracy, latency, repeatability

Design work compared IMU response, sample rate, sensor lag, fixture geometry, mounting alignment, and competing spring-based measurement methods.

User Problem
Completed laundry gets forgotten

The product addresses the common failure mode where the user leaves the laundry area, the cycle finishes, and clothing remains in the washer because the completion cue is no longer noticed.

Product Architecture
Machine sensor + remote receiver

A compact sensor clips or mounts to the washer, while a separate receiver remains in the user's living area to make completion status persistent and visible.

UX Strategy
No phone app

The design deliberately avoids accounts, phone pairing, application setup, and transient mobile notifications. The physical receiver itself carries the reminder state.

Product Communication
Simple physical story

The concept was developed around an immediately understandable sequence: laundry is forgotten, the receiver visibly reminds the user, and recognition triggers action without another digital workflow.

Product Concept
Lighting as architectural/deck art

Developed as one product in a line of innovative consumer lighting products intended to combine illumination with a distinctive decorative physical form.

Mechanical Development
3D product definition

Created 3D drawings to move from concept toward manufacturable hardware and to define physical geometry for outside fabrication processes.

Manufacturing / Vendors
Laser machining + finishing

Worked with Seattle-area laser-machining and coating specialists, iterating the product design to improve manufacturability and finish quality.

Commercialization
Brand → web → Amazon → advertising

Created a web presence and brand, established Amazon Seller Central capability, and used Google AdWords to drive customer traffic and test market interest.

Product Definition
Consumer luminaire concept

Developed as part of a broader line of innovative consumer lighting products rather than as a one-off prototype, requiring both technical definition and product presentation.

Design Method
3D geometry before fabrication

Used 3D product drawings to establish form, dimensions, assembly intent, and the information needed to communicate with fabrication vendors.

Design for Manufacture
Supplier-driven iteration

Worked directly with laser-machining and coating specialists to evolve the design based on practical manufacturing and finishing constraints.

Go-to-Market Work
Product, brand, channel, demand test

Supported the lighting line with branding, web presence, Amazon Seller Central, and paid search traffic—extending engineering work into commercialization and customer acquisition.

Problem / Concept
Aggregate fragmented messaging

Customer Discovery
Survey the problem before building

Product / UX Validation

Business / IP

Filed a provisional patent, reviewed the business plan with local business leaders, and interviewed multiple software vendors as part of the build-versus-source path toward an MVP.
